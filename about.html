About InternPortal

InternPortal is a Student Internship Placement Portal designed to connect students with internship opportunities from different companies and career fields.

Students can create their profile, select their preferred course and internship field, explore companies and find opportunities that match their career goals.

Our Mission

🎯 Career Opportunities

Help students discover internships related to their education and career interests.

🏢 Industry Connection

Create a bridge between educational institutions, students and companies.

🚀 Skill Development

Encourage students to gain practical experience and become job-ready.

How InternPortal Works

1. Register

Students create their internship profile.

2. Explore

Students explore companies and available internships.

3. Apply

Students choose an internship matching their profile.
